Not a chatbot. An assistant.

Six things that make it feel less like software and more like someone working with you.

Yours, and only yours

It learns your habits, your clients and your day. It has a personality, a calm British voice and a dry sense of humour, sir.

Feels like a real conversation

No button to hold. Just talk. Interrupt it mid-sentence, and it knows when you've finished speaking.

Runs on your laptop

It hears you on your own machine. Simple commands never leave it. Your memories, chats and files stay yours.

Does real work

Background jobs while you keep talking: store sections built and checked, videos made, mail drafted, reminders kept.

Controls your desktop

Apps, windows, music, volume, screenshots. It types for you and can look at your screen when you ask.

Powerful, never reckless

Anything risky waits for your OK. It writes email drafts but never sends them. You stay in charge.

Your workspace

Everything it's doing, at a glance

Ask for something big and it becomes a job that runs in the background. You see every step as it happens, and you can keep chatting.

  • Live progress, step by step
  • Pause, resume or talk about any job
  • Screenshots of the result before you approve
Your day

Calendar and reminders in one timeline

Your meetings and your reminders, side by side. It nags you gently until it's done, and tells you about your day the first time you say hello.

  • A morning brief, by voice
  • Reminders that repeat
  • Add or move events by just asking
Your inbox

It reads your mail, so you don't have to

It picks out the emails that need a reply, reads them out, and drafts the answer. Sending is always your click.

  • Needs-reply first, newsletters out of the way
  • Drafts in your own Gmail
  • Never sends on its own
Your memory

It remembers what you tell it

Your preferences and the people and projects in your life. You can see, edit or delete every memory, and it stays on your laptop.

  • Remembers on its own, tells you when it does
  • Search, edit, forget
  • Learns the words it mishears

How it works

Simple on the outside. A lot of care on the inside.

You talk

It listens on your laptop, cancels its own voice and hears you even in a noisy room.

It thinks

Quick questions get a quick answer. Hard ones go to a stronger brain. Simple commands need no AI at all.

It acts

It does the work itself or hands it to the best tool for the job, then shows you what it did.

You decide

Anything that matters waits for your OK. Nothing is published, sent or deleted behind your back.
