Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own laptop. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar events): to show upcoming events, and to add, move or delete events when the owner asks and approves.
  • Gmail (read-only and compose): to read and search the owner's email and to write drafts. Jarvis cannot send email.

Where the data goes

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's laptop, in a private file.
  • To answer a request, parts of an email or an event may be sent to the AI model the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude API), only as part of that request.
  • Google data is never sold, never shared with anyone else and never used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact forms

If you fill in the contact form on this site, your name and email are used only to reply to you, and never shared.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time with Disconnect in Jarvis's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
